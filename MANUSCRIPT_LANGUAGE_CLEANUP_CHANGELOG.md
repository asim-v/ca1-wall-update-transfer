# Manuscript language-cleanup changelog

Date: 2026-08-02

Scope: wording only. Analyses, numerical values, citations, figures, section
order, and scientific conclusions were held fixed.

| Purpose | Original wording | Revised wording |
|---|---|---|
| Bound the abstract conclusion to measured variables | “constrained by position and behavioral state” | “weaker than exact-location recurrence, spatially constrained, and partly coupled to speed and licking” |
| Simplify the spatial-alternative sentence | “Among every geometry- and support-admissible source exactly 25 cm from each target...” | “Against all admissible sources at the same 25 cm distance...” |
| Clarify the nonlinear reward sentence | “reduced this statistic ... while improving ...” | “the statistic was ... with only a 1.03% improvement ...” |
| Remove premature estimator notation | “a separate multi-geometry estimator rather than the \(u-o\) contrast” | “constructed from wall and open training states across multiple geometries” |

| Bound the mechanistic interpretation | “Stable relative tuning is a direct mechanistic explanation” | “Stable relative tuning is a parsimonious candidate explanation” |
| Distinguish transfer from portability | “invariant transformation” | “position-independent portability” |
| Tie reward coupling to measured variables | “shared with behavior” / “Behavior is part of this ... geometry” | “shared with speed and licking” / “Speed and licking are part of this ... geometry” |
| Name the wall adjustment precisely | “behavior-adjusted” | “measured-covariate-adjusted” or “covariate-adjusted” |
