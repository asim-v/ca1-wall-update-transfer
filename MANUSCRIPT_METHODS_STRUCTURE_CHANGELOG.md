# Methods structure changelog

Date: 2026-08-02

The Methods hierarchy was reduced from 17 numbered subsections to five. No
method text was deleted or reordered.

| Previous numbered blocks | Revised organization |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | 4.1 Dataset description, retaining the two dataset subheadings |
| 4.2--4.7 | 4.2 Boundary representation and primary estimators |
| 4.8--4.13 | 4.3 Boundary controls and adjudication |

The former short numbered subsections are now unnumbered run-in headings inside
their corresponding methodological block. Analyses, equations, numerical
values, citations, eligibility rules, negative controls, and stopping
conditions are unchanged.
